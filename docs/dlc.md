# DLC specials — `dlc.html`

> Part of the Distrilight tools suite — start from `CLAUDE.md` in the repo root, which
> holds the hard rules, the map of the repo and how to check a change. This file is
> the depth for one tool: read it before you change `dlc.html`.

`dlc.html` is the **DLC specials** tool: paste a supplier's specification text for a fixture that is *not* in the product data, add photos, and it produces a DLC product sheet. Unlike the rest of the suite this one carries the **DLC** house style, taken from the supplied design (`DLC Productblad.indd`, example Norma Track) — a black header band with the DLC wordmark, Poppins for the headings and Helvetica for the tables. Every measurement in the `M` table at the top of `bouwBlad()` was read out of that PDF rather than eyeballed, and `y` there is a **baseline**, not a box top (`tekst()` converts) — that is what keeps the measured numbers usable as-is.

**The photos go in five slots** (`BEELDVAKKEN`, numbered as in the user's sketch of September 2026): 1 Hoofdfoto top right, 4 Detail 1 and 5 Detail 2 side by side under it, 2 Afmetingen under those, and 3 Toepassing left, under the table. They are not fixed boxes an image floats in (that was the first version, and the images drifted apart by 55–77 pt): `fotoKolom()` and `toepassingPlek()` lay them out on one grid, with the numbers in `FOTO`, all measured on the sheet. The photo column runs from one column gap right of the table to the right edge of the DLC logo (323.8–559.9); that gap equals the left margin (35.4) and the logo edge sits at the same margin from the right, so the page is symmetric. The column starts at the cap height of BESTELGEGEVENS (141), so photos and data begin on one line. Each image gets the full column width and the height its own shape gives it; the next follows one gutter (`goot`, 12 pt) lower, and the gutter between 4 and 5 is the same. Too tall: the hoofdfoto and the drawing get narrower and centred (nothing is cut off a product or a dimension drawing), unless the hoofdfoto is only just too tall (≤ 8 %, a 3:4 portrait), where losing some background beats edges 7 pt off those below; the details and the toepassing are cropped (`metUitsnede` + `beeldVullend`). 4 and 5 are twins — same width and height, the height following their average shape within 4:5 portrait to 4:3 landscape. The toepassing is as wide as the table; if the top of a photo on the right lies 16–64 pt under the table it starts on that line, and if its bottom can land on the bottom of a photo on the right for at most 25 % cropping (never taller than square) it ends there, so the two columns finish together. The right column is always on page 1; the toepassing goes under the table wherever that ends, and to a new page when less than 100 pt is left. `controleer-logica.mjs` checks the grid (margins, gutters, twins, caps, the snapping) on the real functions. The panel shows the five slots in the same arrangement; an image is set by clicking (file picker), Ctrl+V or dropping a file on the slot, and **dragging an image onto another slot swaps the two**. A file dropped next to a slot is swallowed rather than letting the browser open it and leave the special. **Older specials** had a hoofdfoto plus a row of images at the bottom: `beeldenNaarVakken()` moves that row, in legend order, into the empty slots 2–5 when the special is opened, and whatever does not fit stays in `S.beelden`, shown under "Nog niet geplaatst" to drag into a slot or remove — it is not drawn on the sheet. `controleer-logica.mjs` checks the migration and the swap on the real code.

The header band is a JPEG lifted from the supplied PDF and baked in as `merk/dlc-kopbalk.jpg` → `MERK_BEELDEN.dlcKopbalk`. The catalogue is the fifty fields the suppliers we buy from actually publish, in six groups — the four of the supplied design plus `Algemeen` (brand, category, EAN, packaging, lamp holder) and `Sensoren`. **A field with no value is left off the sheet, and a group whose fields are all empty disappears with its heading**, so a sparse special still comes out looking like the reference. Field groups are fixed (that is what makes the sheet recognisable); within a group fields can be emptied, renamed or added. The DLC tool has its **own** `VELDMAP` — it shares the reader with the vergelijker but not the field map — so a label spelling added in one does not reach the other; keep the two in step when a supplier turns up a new wording (the housing colour is the worked example: `kleur behuizing` / `behuizingskleur` / `kastkleur` / `housing colour` / `body colour` / `finish`). **An empty field is left off the sheet, and a group whose fields are all empty disappears with its heading.** A special is a one-off, so "Special opslaan" saves it the same way `armaturenboek.html`/`index.html`/`vergelijking.html` save a project: it bakes the current state into a self-contained copy of `dlc.html` itself (`slaSpecialOp()`), which reopens fully filled in when double-clicked — there is no separate "Openen" button or JSON file.
